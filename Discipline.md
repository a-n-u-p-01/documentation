### **1. Start Small & Build Habit**

- Pick **1–2 core tasks** per day (e.g., DSA + project feature).
    
- Use **time blocks**: 60–90 minutes of focused work, then short break.
    
- Keep a **visual tracker**: a calendar or habit app.
    

> **Tip:** Focus on _sticking to the schedule_, not perfection.

---

### **2. Daily Structure**

1. **Morning (30–60 min):** Light planning & review yesterday’s work
    
2. **Focus Session 1 (60–90 min):** DSA or learning topic
    
3. **Short Break (5–10 min)**
    
4. **Focus Session 2 (60–90 min):** Project / coding practice
    
5. **Review & Notes (15–20 min):** Summarize what you learned
    

> **Consistency > Quantity** — even 2–3 hours daily consistently beats occasional long sessions.

---

### **3. Avoid Distractions**

- Phone on silent / do-not-disturb
    
- Use website blockers for social media
    
- Work in a **dedicated space** (desk, not bed)
    

---

### **4. Accountability**

- Track tasks in **notebook or app**
    
- Share progress with a friend or mentor daily
    
- Weekly review: Celebrate small wins
    

---

### **5. Mindset & Motivation**

- Focus on **small wins every day**
    
- Remind yourself: “I am building discipline, the job will follow.”
    
- Reward yourself for consistency (coffee, short walk, small treat)
    

---

### **6. Weekly Reflection**

- Every Sunday, answer:
    
    - Did I complete my planned sessions?
        
    - What made me procrastinate?
        
    - How can I improve next week?
        

>**Key:** Discipline is a muscle — it strengthens **only through repetition**, not motivation.
